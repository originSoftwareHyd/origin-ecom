Assume you are hired as the **lead SEO, GEO, AEO strategist and organic growth consultant for Origin Ecommerce**, with the goal of making it one of the most visible and competitive providers in its niche across Saudi Arabia.

I don't want basic SEO improvements or a generic checklist. I want **exceptional, research-driven work that can give us a genuine competitive advantage and consistently bring qualified clients to our website.**

### 1. Research Before You Build
Thoroughly research the current Saudi Arabian market:
- Identify our strongest competitors, their SEO strategies, keyword coverage, landing pages, backlink profiles, content strategies, and positioning.
- Analyse search demand, keyword difficulty, search intent, commercial value, regional variations, and gaps competitors are failing to address.
- Identify high-intent searches across Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Khobar, and other relevant Saudi cities.
- Explore opportunities competitors are overlooking, including long-tail keywords, underserved services, local search, Arabic queries, industry-specific searches, and AI-powered discovery.
- Determine which opportunities are most likely to generate actual enquiries and paying clients, rather than just traffic.

Use current evidence wherever possible. Distinguish verified search data from estimates and strategic hypotheses. Do not fabricate search volumes, rankings, or competitor metrics.

### 2. Develop a Winning Organic Growth Strategy
Don't blindly follow conventional SEO practices. Evaluate different approaches and select the strongest strategy for our business.

Consider:
- High-intent service pages targeting specific commercial searches.
- Dedicated city landing pages, such as IT solutions in Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam, wherever supported by genuine search demand.
- Industry-specific landing pages for sectors with strong commercial potential.
- Comparison pages, solution pages, educational content, and problem-specific pages that capture customers at different stages of their buying journey.
- Local SEO, Google Business Profile opportunities, authoritative backlinks, digital PR, topical authority, and brand/entity optimisation.
- GEO and AEO strategies to improve our chances of being discovered, recommended, and cited by AI-powered search platforms.

**Don't create pages simply to target more keywords.** Every landing page must have a distinct search intent, compelling value proposition, useful original content, and a clear conversion objective. Avoid doorway pages, keyword cannibalisation, duplicated city content, and manipulative link-building tactics.

### 3. Build a High-Converting Website
Turn the research into actual implementation within the existing codebase.

- Preserve the existing UI, design system, theme, and functionality unless a specific change is necessary.
- Rewrite weak content with persuasive, credible, locally relevant messaging.
- Create dedicated landing pages for the highest-value opportunities you identify.
- Optimise titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal links, schema markup, crawlability, indexation, page speed, and Core Web Vitals.
- Build strong conversion paths with compelling CTAs, enquiry forms, WhatsApp contact options where appropriate, and clear business value propositions.
- Ensure every page is technically sound, mobile-friendly, accessible, and designed to convert visitors into qualified leads.
- Implement a scalable content and internal-linking architecture that strengthens the entire domain rather than treating every page in isolation.

### 4. Win in Search and AI Discovery
Build a unified strategy across traditional search engines, local search, and AI-powered answer engines.

Optimise for Google Search, Google Maps where applicable, ChatGPT search, Gemini, Perplexity, and other relevant discovery platforms. Focus on useful, original content, clear business entities, trustworthy information, relevant citations, structured data, and strong topical coverage. Never promise guaranteed rankings or AI citations.

### 5. Prioritise by Business Impact
Create a prioritised execution plan based on commercial intent, opportunity size, competition, implementation effort, and expected impact.

Identify:
- The highest-priority keywords and landing pages to build first.
- Quick wins and underserved search opportunities.
- The technical and content improvements with the greatest potential impact.
- A realistic 30/60/90-day roadmap.
- The tracking and measurement setup needed to evaluate qualified organic leads, conversion rates, rankings, and revenue contribution.

### Execution Rules

1. Research first, form a strategy, and then implement it. Do not start building pages based on assumptions.
2. Inspect the entire existing codebase and understand the business before making changes.
3. Use reliable research tools and available data sources. Clearly flag any data that requires access to Google Search Console, Google Analytics, keyword research platforms, or competitor backlink tools.
4. Implement the highest-value improvements directly. Do not stop after producing a strategy document.
5. Validate the implementation, preserve existing functionality, and document completed work versus anything requiring external access or manual configuration.

**My ultimate objective is simple: when a potential customer in Saudi Arabia searches for the services we offer, I want Origin Ecommerce to have the strongest possible chance of being discovered, trusted, contacted, and chosen over competitors.**

Think like a strategist accountable for business growth, not an SEO freelancer ticking boxes. Challenge my assumptions, identify opportunities I haven't considered, prioritise what will actually generate customers, and execute at an exceptional standard. You have the freedom to choose the strategy—justify your decisions with research and deliver the work.
