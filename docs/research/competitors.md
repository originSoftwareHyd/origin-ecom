# Competitive Intelligence: Saudi Digital Agency SERPs

Prepared for Origin Ecommerce, 2026-10-16.

## 0. Method and limitations (read first)

- **Tooling.** All searches used a web-search tool that runs from the US. It is **not** Google.com.sa and is not run from inside KSA. Its results are not a Google SERP, so "appeared in results" here does **not** mean "ranks #N on Google in Riyadh". Real Saudi SERPs are personalised and location-dependent, and for "near me"/city queries they are dominated by the Google Maps local pack, which this tool cannot see.
- **What this report contains.** For each query it lists the domains and page types the tool returned, in the order returned. That order is a rough signal of which page types search engines treat as relevant. It is not a ranking.
- **No metrics were fabricated.** This report has no search volumes, keyword difficulty, backlink counts or traffic estimates. Before committing budget, validate with:
  - **Semrush or Ahrefs (database: SA):** volume and KD for every target keyword; the top-10 for each query with SERP features (local pack, PAA, video); referring domains for Sendan Tech, Arabi Brand, United SEO, art4muslim, Method, PUIUX, RedRock and Codilar.
  - **GSC:** once the site is live, impressions by query and country.
  - **Manual Google.com.sa checks from a KSA IP or VPN:** local-pack presence for city queries.
- **Self-reported claims.** Competitor facts in section 2 come from pages fetched on 2026-10-16. Claims such as "100% compliant", project counts and ratings are the competitors' own statements and were not verified.

### 1c. Page-type pattern (synthesis)

1. **English commercial queries are owned by directories and foreign agencies.** Clutch, GoodFirms, TechBehemoths, Sortlist, DesignRush, The Manifest and Semrush Agencies fill them, alongside UAE- and India-based agencies with `/ksa/` or `-riyadh` city pages and parasite listicles on third-party domains. **Saudi-based agencies barely appear in English.** The exceptions are Sendan (ERP), SEO Sherpa (SEO; Dubai-HQ) and RedRock/Codilar (via blogs).
2. **Arabic commercial queries are owned by:**
   - the platforms themselves (salla.com/blog, zid.sa);
   - the Hsoub marketplaces (Khamsat, Mostaql);
   - Saudi and Egyptian agency **dedicated service pages** (`/salla-design/`, `/zid-store/`);
   - **city pages** (Zeinahost, Younex, Tahwal);
   - **self-ranking "أفضل 10 شركات … في [city]" blog posts**.
3. **Compliance and ERP informational queries are owned by SaaS vendors' blogs:** ClearTax, Wafeq, Daftra, Qoyod, Dafater.
4. **Programmatic city × industry × service pages work** even when thin. Sendan returned 10 of 10 results for the ERPNext query.

## 2. Direct competitors (8 profiled)

### 2.1 Sendan Technology: sendantech.com (ERP / ZATCA)
- **Positioning:** "Certified ERPNext Partner, Implementer & Developer in Riyadh". It sells turnkey ZATCA Phase 2 connectors for ERPNext, Odoo, SAP, Oracle, Dynamics, Zoho and others. [Riyadh page](https://www.sendantech.com/en-US/riyadh/erpnext), [ZATCA page](https://www.sendantech.com/en-SA/zatca-e-invoicing)
- **Page types:** platform pages (/en-SA/erpnext, /odoo, /sap…); industry hub (/en-SA/industries, about 20 industries); **programmatic city × platform × industry × service URLs** (e.g. `/en-SA/dammam/erpnext/healthcare/crm-implementation`, `/en-SA/khobar/erpnext/finance/migration`); Insights blog; **free tools** (ZATCA QR decoder, Phase 2 readiness / wave checker); published **pricing tiers** (SAR 9,500–18,000 / 22,000–45,000 / 55,000–110,000+).
- **Languages:** English only (en-SA and en-US). No ar-SA pages were found.
- **Trust signals:** a self-declared "Certified Partner" badge; "100%" clearance and "200+ devices" claims with no named clients or certifying body.
- **CTAs:** WhatsApp with prefilled messages, phone, a one-field email form, and a floating widget.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Templated, thin copy: the same sentence repeats with the industry name swapped, stock photos repeat, and an "Industry Demos" section is empty.
  - The ZATCA wave table is stale. It shows waves 13–16 as "current" while Wave 24 (deadline 30 Jun 2026) has already passed and a Wave 25 has reportedly been announced.
  - **There is no Arabic content.**
  - It is not visible in the Frappe partner directory results I saw (verify).

## 4. Gaps Origin can own

Each gap cites the result set where it was observed.

1. **English-language Salla and Zid service pages.**
   - **Evidence:** "Salla store design" returned only Haraj ads and the Salla help center; "Zid store design agency Saudi" returned only Zid funding news. Every Salla/Zid agency page found is Arabic-only (Arabi Brand, Younex, Mrhbaa).
   - **Who it serves:** expat-run and foreign-brand merchants entering KSA who search in English. Origin's site is already en-SA.
   - **Pages:** `/salla-store-design`, `/zid-store-design`, `/salla-to-shopify-migration`.
2. **Commerce-to-ERP-to-ZATCA integration: the strongest structural gap.**
   - **Evidence:**
     - "Salla ZATCA e-invoice integration ERPNext store sync" returned only ECOSIRE app listings and **nothing about Salla**.
     - Arabic "ربط متجر سلة مع نظام ERP" returned only **Odoo** connectors (Salla app store, apps.odoo.com, katheeb.net, origami.sa), **with no ERPNext connector**.
     - Sendan (ERP) has no e-commerce offer; Salla/Zid agencies have no ERP or ZATCA offer.
   - **Pages:** "Salla + ERPNext integration", "Zid + ERPNext integration", "Shopify + ERPNext for KSA", "WooCommerce ZATCA Phase 2" (EN + AR).
3. **ERPNext and ZATCA content in Arabic.**
   - **Evidence:**
     - The Sendan pages that monopolise "ERPNext Saudi Arabia" are **English-only**.
     - The Arabic "نظام ERP السعودية" and "ربط الفوترة الإلكترونية المرحلة الثانية" results contain **no ERPNext implementer at all**: they are Daftra, Qoyod, Wazen and Dafater blogs.
   - **Pages:** "تطبيق ERPNext في السعودية", "ربط ERPNext بمنصة فاتورة".
4. **Current, wave-specific ZATCA content.**
   - **Evidence:**
     - Sendan's wave table stops at waves 13–16.
     - ClearTax's wave articles disagree on thresholds (3bn vs 500M SAR for wave 2).
     - Wave 24 (SAR 375k, deadline 30 Jun 2026) is covered by EY, Wafeq and Beancount, not by agencies. [EY](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2025-2023-saudi-arabia-announces-24th-wave-of-phase-2-e-invoicing-integration), [Wafeq](https://www.wafeq.com/en-sa/tax-and-reporting/zatca-wave-24:-who-must-comply-by-june-30-2026-and-exactly-what-you-need-to-do)
     - Wave 25 (SAR 187,500, due 1 Feb 2027) appears in **a single source only** (Beancount, IT locale). [Beancount](https://beancount.io/it/blog/2026/10/06/saudi-arabia-zatca-wave-24-e-invoicing-sme-guide)
   - **Opportunity:** a maintained "ZATCA Phase 2 waves tracker" plus a "Wave 25 readiness for ERPNext / Salla merchants" page, in EN and AR.
   - **Caveat:** verify every wave against zatca.gov.sa before publishing.
5. **Platform comparisons from a Saudi agency that builds on all of them.**
   - **Evidence:** in Arabic, "مقارنة سلة وزد وشوبيفاي" returns many blogs (storeino, tmt3, a-amaq, kfagency). In English, results are RedRock (pricing as of Q1 2025), wmtips (data tool), LetsBot (chatbot vendor), Zid's own switcher page and foreign Shopify blogs. [RedRock](https://www.redrock.com.sa/blog/salla-vs-zid-vs-shopify-saudi-arabia/), [Zid switcher](https://zid.sa/en/switchers/zid-x-shopify/)
   - **Opportunity:** few comparisons are written by a builder covering Salla, Zid, Shopify, WooCommerce *and* custom builds, with 2026 pricing and a ZATCA/ERP angle. This is a medium-difficulty gap: English is the less crowded side.
6. **Migration guides in both directions and in Arabic.**
   - **Evidence:** "migrate Salla to Shopify OR نقل متجر من سلة إلى زد" returned **English results only** (powercommerce, praella, magnetoitsolutions, codilar, lucidly). No Arabic migration content appeared. Salla↔Zid, WooCommerce→Salla and Salla→custom were not covered by any result.
7. **Cost and pricing guides with real numbers.**
   - **Evidence:** the Arabic "تكلفة تصميم متجر إلكتروني في السعودية 2026" results are many blogs with wildly inconsistent ranges (2,000 to 50,000+ SAR). In English, competitors that publish prices (art4muslim, PUIUX, Sendan) get cited and appear.
   - **Opportunity:** a transparent, bilingual cost guide per platform and per service (store, app, ERPNext, chatbot). This is low-hanging fruit for an English version.
8. **City pages backed by real substance.**
   - **Evidence:**
     - Arabic Jeddah and Riyadh results reward city pages (Zeinahost, Younex, Tahwal).
     - English Dammam and Khobar results return **only directories**: no agency city page appeared at all.
     - English Riyadh, Jeddah and app-development results are taken by **UAE and Indian agencies** (Red Berries, Promatics, AppSquadz, OwlClaw).
   - **Opportunity:** a Saudi agency with a real local address, case studies and a Google Business Profile can beat both.
   - **Caution:** Sendan shows thin templated pages can index, but they carry quality and spam risk. Build fewer, deeper city pages.
9. **B2B / wholesale e-commerce.**
   - **Evidence:** "B2B ecommerce platform Saudi Arabia wholesale portal development" returned news (Sary), a StoreHippo forum post and a foreign agency portfolio. No Saudi agency service page appeared.
   - **Opportunity:** a B2B portal page (ERPNext-backed price lists and credit terms, plus ZATCA B2B *clearance*, which differs from B2C *reporting*).
10. **An agency-built "WhatsApp AI agent for Salla/Zid stores".**
    - **Evidence:** "شات بوت واتساب" and "بوت واتساب لمتاجر سلة وزد" return **SaaS tools** (Whatsloop, Atmet 99 SAR/month, Mando, Thikaa, 7ayak, Salla app-store apps). English "WhatsApp chatbot Saudi" returns SaaS vendor blogs. Only Younex (Egypt) and automation-ksa.com present it as a service.
    - **Opportunity:** position on custom AI connected to orders, ERP stock and Arabic dialect handling, with handoff to staff. Avoid competing on SaaS price.
    - **Caution:** "AI chatbot Saudi Arabia" itself is a news SERP owned by HUMAIN, so do not target that head term.
11. **Industry-specific store pages.**
    - **Evidence:** no competitor profiled has real industry pages for e-commerce: Sendan's are ERP-only and templated, art4muslim's sector links point to "#", and Method and PUIUX have none.
    - **Candidates** (validate volume first): perfumes/oud, abayas/fashion, coffee roasters, cosmetics, B2B food supply.
